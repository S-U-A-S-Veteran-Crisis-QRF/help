## Slide 2 — The Problem

**17**

Veterans lost to suicide every single day.
*(Source: VA National Veteran Suicide Prevention Annual Report)*

> "The enemy after discharge isn't foreign. It's waking up with no formation, no unit, no mission."

- **1 in 3 veterans** report significant social isolation post-service.
- The highest-risk window is the first two years after discharge.
- No standard of care reaches them where they are.

Crisis hotlines help at the edge. They don't prevent the months of silence that lead there.

---

## Slide 3 — The Isolation Cycle

**THE CYCLE THAT KILLS.**

**01 — DISCHARGE**
The unit dissolves. Years of structure, identity, and belonging disappear overnight. There is no offboarding from war.

**02 — SILENCE**
No squad. No formation. Civilian peers don't understand. Reaching out feels like admitting defeat.

**03 — ALONE**
Isolation deepens into depression. By the time crisis arrives, the veteran has already been unreachable for months.

---

## Slide 4 — The Solution

**YOUR UNIT, IN YOUR POCKET.**

The SUAS QRF App is the mobile companion to suasqrf.org — veteran-first, built for the way veterans actually live: peer connection, shared events, and immediate crisis access, all in one place.

| Pillar | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Connect** | Peer-matched with veterans who've been there — same branch, same era, same struggle. |
| **Gather** | Find events near you. Cookouts, hikes, surf sessions. RSVP in two taps. |
| **Respond** | 988 always one tap away. No buried menus. No hunting. Just there. |

---

## Slide 5 — Five Features

**1. Peer Connect**
Match by branch, era, and situation. Real conversations — not a helpline, not a forum. A squad.

**2. Event Finder**
BBQs, camping, hunting, surfing, skydiving. Find what's near you. RSVP instantly.

**3. Crisis Line** ← always on
Persistent 988 shortcut at the bottom of every screen. One tap. No friction. Always on.

**4. Resource Navigator**
Benefits, housing, mental health — filtered to your situation. No VA maze required.

**5. Mission Feed**
Live updates from the SUAS community and website. The unit stays loud.

---

## Slide 6 — Website Connection

**SAME COMMUNITY. EVERY SCREEN.**

The SUAS website at suasqrf.org is the home base — credibility, content, organization. The app is the field unit. They share one event calendar, one community feed, and one mission.

A veteran finds SUAS on Facebook or Google, lands on the site, and downloads the app. App users volunteer, donate, and share — driving traffic back. The loop closes. No veteran falls through the gap between screens.
