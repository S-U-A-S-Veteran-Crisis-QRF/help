# Commercial Script — Hackathon Pitch
**S.U.A.S. Veteran Crisis QRF × Hacker Dojo**
**"One Button. One Service."**
Duration: ~60 seconds

---

## SCENE 1 — NIGHT SKY (0:00–0:08)

**[VISUAL]**
Slow pull back from a single star in the night sky.
More stars appear. Hundreds. Thousands.
The Milky Way stretches horizon to horizon — vast, overwhelming, beautiful.

**[NARRATION — low, steady voice]**
> "There are thousands of services built for veterans.
> Benefits. Housing. Mental health. Jobs. Legal help.
> They're all out there."

---

## SCENE 2 — THE VETERAN (0:08–0:22)

**[VISUAL]**
A veteran stands alone in an open field at night.
Still. Looking up.
The stars reflect in their eyes.

**[NARRATION]**
> "But most veterans never reach them.
> The paperwork. The phone trees. The waiting.
> It's like reaching for a star."

**[VISUAL]**
The veteran slowly extends one hand toward the sky —
fingers spread, stars between them.
Out of reach.

---

## SCENE 3 — THE PHONE (0:22–0:38)

**[VISUAL]**
They reach into their pocket.
Pull out a phone.
Hold it up toward the stars.

The screen glows.

One button. Clean. Simple.

A single tap.

**[VISUAL]**
The stars on the screen — each one a service — begin to move.
They flow down from the sky, through the phone, into one place.
Housing. Healthcare. Crisis support. Benefits. Community.
All of it, gathered.

**[SOUND]**
A soft, clean chime. Done.

**[NARRATION]**
> "S.U.A.S. Veteran Crisis QRF and Hacker Dojo built this
> at the hackathon.
> One app. Built in a weekend. For the people who served."

---

## SCENE 4 — TAGLINE (0:38–0:52)

**[VISUAL]**
The veteran lowers the phone.
Looks at the screen.
Looks back up at the sky — and smiles.

The stars are still there.
But now they're reachable.

**[TITLE CARD — full screen, bold, white on black]**

```
ONE BUTTON.
ONE SERVICE.
```

---

## SCENE 5 — CLOSE (0:52–1:00)

**[VISUAL]**
Logos fade in, side by side:
**S.U.A.S. Veteran Crisis QRF** | **Hacker Dojo**

**[NARRATION]**
> "If you're a veteran in crisis — you don't have to search the stars alone."

**[LOWER THIRD]**
```
Veterans Crisis Line — Dial 988, Press 1
suasqrf.org
```

**[FADE TO BLACK]**

---

## Production Notes

- Shoot at golden hour or blue hour for the field/sky scene — a phone flashlight or practical light source on the actor's face reads well on camera
- The phone UI should be minimal: dark background, one large button, clean icon per service category
- Narration tone: not sad, not hype — grounded, like a friend who served
- Tagline reads best in silence — let it land before the logo
- 988 Crisis Line required on screen per SUAS policy
